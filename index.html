Buying on the Island

  • No stamp duty is charged on Isle of Man property purchases, unlike the UK. Confirm with your advocate.
  • Budget for advocate's fees, a survey, and Land Registry fees on top of the price.
  • Your yearly rates bill from the local authority is extra to the mortgage.
  • Manx NI and tax differ from the UK, so a UK mortgage calculator will give the wrong take-home pay.
